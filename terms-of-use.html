Sync & reminders

Eligible Pro settings, goals, plans, and awards can sync through your private iCloud account. Steps does not sync activity measurements or body measurements through this feature. Sync depends on account access, connectivity, and service availability and may be delayed. It does not guarantee complete activity or streak restoration. Reset Local Data leaves the cloud copy intact, which can restore later.

Optional Daily Progress and Goal Reminder notifications depend on permission, readable activity, and iOS scheduling. Delivery time and up-to-date content cannot be guaranteed. They are general fitness reminders.

Understanding your activity

Steps helps you follow activity and set Goal Day and Recovery Day targets. Counts, distance, and energy may be estimated, delayed, or incomplete. Results depend on your device, permissions, data sources, and synchronization. Apple Health Active Energy can include activity beyond walking; estimated walking energy is approximate. The hourly chart uses iPhone motion data and may differ from a daily total that includes Apple Watch activity.

Today can change as sources update. Completed historical summaries are finalized locally and can differ from later Health corrections or delayed Watch synchronization. Goal and plan edits apply to today and later dates rather than rewriting saved past results. Missing data is distinct from zero activity.

Steps is for general fitness information. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Do not rely on it for medical or safety-critical decisions. Choose activities appropriate for you and seek professional advice for health concerns.
