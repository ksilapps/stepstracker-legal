Support for Steps

A few checks usually explain missing activity. Start with the data source shown in the app, then check the related permission.

Steps: Walking Tracker · iPhone · App version 0.1.0 · Updated October 5, 2026

Apple Health access

Open Steps → Settings → Apple Health and request access. In the iPhone Health app, review the app's permissions and allow Steps, Walking + Running Distance, and Active Energy if you want those values displayed. Health access is read only. The app cannot tell whether a particular Health read permission was denied.

Motion & Fitness access

Open Steps → Settings → Motion & Fitness. If access is denied, use that row to open iOS Settings and allow motion access for Steps. Motion & Fitness provides iPhone pedometer steps when a readable Apple Health total is unavailable and supplies the hourly iPhone view.

Steps are not updating

Check the source and observation time near your activity details. Carry your iPhone for Motion & Fitness steps, or check that Apple Health has a current step total. Open Steps again or return it to the foreground to refresh. If the app shows saved data, fresh data may be temporarily unavailable. Also confirm the date and time on your phone.

Apple Watch steps are missing

Steps does not read directly from Apple Watch. Watch activity can appear in the daily Apple Health total after the Watch syncs with your iPhone, Apple Health has recorded it, and you have allowed Steps to read step count. Check the Health app's Steps chart first, then reopen Steps. The hourly movement view uses iPhone Motion data and may differ from a daily total that includes Watch data.

Distance or energy looks different

Steps uses measured distance where available and estimates it from steps otherwise. Apple Health Active Energy can include activity beyond walking; an estimated walking energy figure is different. Review the source labels in the app before comparing values.

Resetting saved app data

In Steps → Settings → Data, choose Reset Local Data to remove saved summaries and preferences from Steps. This does not delete Apple Health records or iPhone Motion & Fitness history. You can then reopen the app and grant permissions again as needed.

Contact & policies

There is currently no verified direct support contact published for Steps. The app owner needs to add a contact method here before using this as a complete support channel. For data practices, see the Privacy Policy; for app conditions, see the Terms of Use.

If a permission change does not appear immediately, close and reopen Steps, then check whether the Health app itself contains the expected activity.
