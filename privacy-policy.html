Privacy Policy

A plain language account of how the current version of Steps: Walking Tracker uses activity information.

Overview

Steps shows your walking activity, progress toward goals, and history. The current app has no account, cloud sync, subscription, advertising, or in-app purchase. Its activity processing and saved app data are on your iPhone. The legal and support website is a separate public site; simply viewing it is subject to your browser and GitHub Pages infrastructure.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Steps requests read access only to step count, walking and running distance, and active energy burned. It uses readable step and distance totals to show daily activity and history. Active energy is used for the calorie display; when unavailable, the app may show an estimated walking energy value instead. Apple Health can include information from iPhone and Apple Watch when it has synced and you have allowed access.

Steps does not write data to Apple Health. Health access is optional and controlled by the iOS permission sheet. Apple does not tell apps whether Health read access was denied, so an empty result does not prove that you recorded zero activity. You can change access in the Health app's settings for Steps.

Apple Health data is used for the activity features you see in the app. The app does not use it for advertising or sell it.

Motion & Fitness

Steps also requests Motion & Fitness permission to read iPhone pedometer data through Core Motion. It uses step count and, when available, distance for daily activity; hourly iPhone step counts support the hourly view. The native pedometer can return pace and floors climbed, but the current app does not display or save those values. In development through Expo Go, a fallback pedometer reads step counts.

When a readable Apple Health daily total is available, the app uses that total instead of adding it to the iPhone pedometer total. Otherwise it may use Motion & Fitness data. Distance may be estimated from steps when measured distance is unavailable. Energy estimates can use body weight and height if you enter them, or app defaults.

What is saved on your device

Steps saves app data in local device storage so your settings and previously viewed activity remain available. This includes:

  • Daily summaries such as steps, goal, distance, active or estimated energy, hourly steps where available, data source, date, and observation details.
  • Your goals and plan history, units, appearance and progress style, selected character body type, and any body weight or height you enter.
  • Achievement unlock dates, onboarding completion, and whether you asked to connect Apple Health.

The app may display older saved summaries when fresh source data cannot be read. It does not require your name, email address, or an account.

Transmission and third-party services

In the audited version, app code contains no account backend, cloud synchronization, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or network request that sends activity or preferences to the developer. The app reads from Apple's on-device HealthKit and Core Motion interfaces. iOS and Apple Health are provided by Apple and operate under Apple's own privacy settings and policies.

When you tap a Privacy Policy, Terms, or Support link, the app opens this public website in your browser. That request goes to GitHub Pages, which hosts the website; the app does not attach your step data or saved preferences to the link.

Your choices and retention

You can decline or change Motion & Fitness and Apple Health permissions in iOS. Without permission or readable data, some activity views may be unavailable. The app's Settings screen has Reset Local Data, which removes its saved summaries, preferences, achievements, and onboarding flag. It does not delete records from Apple Health or iPhone Motion & Fitness. Uninstalling the app generally removes its local app storage, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior outside this app's control.

Saved summaries otherwise remain on the device until you reset the app's local data or remove the app. The app does not provide a developer-held account or server copy to delete.

Children, security, and changes

Children

Steps is a general walking utility and is not designed specifically for children. The audited version has no account or direct collection of identifying details from children.

Security

Activity summaries and preferences use the app's local iOS storage. Protect access to your device with iOS security features. No software or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and its date revised. Material changes may also be explained in the app or App Store listing.

Questions and contact

See the Support page for troubleshooting. A direct developer support contact has not yet been published for this app; the owner needs to add a verified contact method before relying on this page for user inquiries.
