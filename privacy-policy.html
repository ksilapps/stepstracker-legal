The basics

Steps: Walking Tracker is an iPhone app developed by ksilapps. It shows your walking activity, goals, history, and achievements. The app does not require an account and does not send your activity or preferences to us.

Activity & permissions

With your permission, Steps reads step count, walking and running distance, and active energy from Apple Health. This can include synced Apple Watch activity. Access is read only: Steps does not add or change Health records.

Motion & Fitness supplies iPhone step counts and available distance, including the hourly view. Steps uses a readable Apple Health daily total when available; otherwise it uses iPhone motion data. It does not add these totals together.

Distance and walking energy may be estimated when measured values are unavailable. Optional height and weight entries help with estimates. Health access is optional, and you can change either permission in iOS.

What stays on your device

Steps saves the following in local app storage:

  • Activity summaries: steps, distance, energy, available hourly counts, dates, and data source details.
  • Your goals and weekly plans, achievements, and app preferences such as units, appearance, and character choice.
  • Height and weight if you enter them, plus setup and permission-request preferences.

Saved activity may remain visible when fresh data is unavailable. The app has no cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting service. We do not sell your activity data or use it for advertising.

Your choices & deletion

Manage Steps’ read access in the Apple Health app, and Motion & Fitness access in iPhone Settings. Some views need these permissions and readable activity data to work.

To remove saved app data, open Steps → Settings → Data → Reset Local Data. This clears activity summaries, preferences, achievements, and setup information. It does not delete Apple Health or iPhone motion records or revoke system permissions. If you continue using Steps, it can read available activity again.

Local data otherwise remains until you reset it or remove the app. iOS backup and restore behavior may affect what is retained or restored. We have no server copy or account to delete. Protect your device with its security settings; no storage method is completely secure.

This website

This website does not use analytics, advertising, or cookies set by us. Opening it from Steps does not include your activity data in the link.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s hosting information and Privacy Statement. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

About this policy

Steps is a general fitness utility and is not designed specifically for children. It does not ask for names, email addresses, or an account.

We will update this page and its date if our data practices change. For help managing data and permissions, visit Support.
